Questions about an installation, a report, or DiffCI's data handling go here. Messages are stored and read by the founder; nothing is emailed automatically, and an email address is optional — leave it if you want a reply.

For a suspected vulnerability, do not use the general form. Email security@diffci.com so the report stays private. For privacy or deletion requests, use the form below and provide a reply address if you want confirmation.
